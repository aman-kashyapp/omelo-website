01 · What is OMELO

Commerce designed around local reality.

OMELO is being built as a digital commerce layer for rural and semi-urban India — bringing everyday products, local businesses and delivery closer to the communities they serve.

Not just another shopping app.

A local commerce network built from the ground up.
01

Everyday commerce

Grocery, food, household essentials and other everyday needs in one experience.

03

Last-mile network

Technology supported by a local operating network built around focused markets.

02 · The challenge

Rural commerce has a different operating reality.

Lower density, longer distances, fragmented supply and different patterns of digital adoption create a different set of requirements from dense urban markets.

01

Distance

Everyday shopping can involve travel when local availability is fragmented.

02

Density

Lower order density changes the economics and operating assumptions of conventional quick commerce.

03

Supply

Local merchants already serve communities, but their inventory and customers can remain digitally disconnected.

04

Trust

Familiar local presence can help bridge the gap between digital technology and everyday community commerce.
